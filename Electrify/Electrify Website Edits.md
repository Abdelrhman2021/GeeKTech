# Electrify Website Redesign & Copy Update Brief

## Objective

Update the current Electrify website so it reflects the company’s current strategic direction.

The website should no longer present Electrify primarily as a battery-swapping startup.

Instead, it should position Electrify as:

> **A white-label mobility operating system for destinations that operate their own vehicles and drivers.**

The new website should communicate that Electrify provides the software and operational infrastructure required to run private mobility networks across destinations such as resorts, gated communities, universities, airports, business parks, industrial zones, hotels, and mixed-use developments.

Battery swapping, charging, IoT, fleet intelligence, and EV infrastructure should be presented as supported capabilities within the wider platform—not as the company’s primary identity.

---

## Confidentiality & Data Handling (Governs Every Section Below)

Treat all customer, operational, technical, and commercial information as confidential by default. Do not expose exact fleet sizes, driver numbers, active-user counts, trip volumes by period, utilization rates, waiting times, uptime, pricing logic, customer workflows, technical architecture, infrastructure providers, API structures, internal roles, or production data.

Use high-level descriptions, approved aggregate figures, sanitized demo data, and generic product mockups instead.

The only currently approved performance figure is **"35,000+ completed rides,"** subject to final customer approval.

This rule overrides any other section in this brief that references specific metrics, counts, or operational detail — those must ship as qualitative/sanitized language or generic mockups, not real figures, even once real figures are known internally.

# 1. Core Positioning Change

## Current perception

The current website makes Electrify appear to be:

- A battery-swapping company
- A hardware-heavy EV infrastructure startup
- A company focused mainly on charging and batteries
- A broad smart-mobility startup without a clearly defined customer

## Desired perception

The updated website should make Electrify appear to be:

- An enterprise mobility software company
- A white-label technology provider
- The operating system behind private mobility networks
- A platform for destinations that own or manage vehicles and drivers
- A scalable, asset-light mobility infrastructure layer
- A company capable of supporting conventional, electric, shared, and battery-swapping fleets

## Recommended positioning statement

Use the following as the central positioning:

> **The Operating System for Private Mobility Networks**

Supporting statement:

> Electrify helps resorts, communities, campuses, airports, and other destinations manage their vehicles, drivers, passengers, payments, operations, and mobility data through one white-label platform.

Alternative approved phrasing:

> **A white-label mobility operating system for destinations that operate their own vehicles and drivers.**
